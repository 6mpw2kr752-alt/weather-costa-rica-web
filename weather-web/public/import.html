Import STM32 readings

Pushes the readings stored on the board (flash dump) into the database, without duplicates.
Earlier readings are timestamped automatically, one every 15 min working backwards.

TTN catch-up

Fetches the last 24 h of readings from TTN and fills in any gaps, without duplicates.

Delete a date range

Permanently erases the readings between two dates. Irreversible.
Both bounds are inclusive. Local time of your computer.
